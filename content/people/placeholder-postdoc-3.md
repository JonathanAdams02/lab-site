Iqra completed her PhD within the framework of the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Innovative Training Network (MSCA-ITN) Horizon 2020 project MULTITOUCH. She is currently a postdoctoral researcher working jointly with Jan Van den Stock at KU Leuven and Giulia Liberati at UCLouvain as part of the Weave project.

Her postdoctoral research focuses on understanding the neural mechanisms underlying thermoception and social cognition, with particular emphasis on the role of the insular cortex. By combining intracerebral EEG recordings from patients with epilepsy and functional MRI (fMRI) in patients with frontotemporal dementia, the project aims to elucidate how the insula processes thermal and socioemotional information, and how neurodegeneration affecting this region contributes to impairments in these functions.
