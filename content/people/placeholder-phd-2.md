**Prof. Jan Van den Stock – Neuropsychology**

Jan Van den Stock is research Professor (BOF-ZAP) at KU Leuven and a clinical neuropsychologist specializing in the assessment of cognitive and behavioural changes in neurological and psychiatric disorders.

His research focuses on the cognitive and neural mechanisms underlying social and emotional functioning, with a particular emphasis on frontotemporal dementia. By combining experimental neuropsychology, neuroimaging, psychophysiology, and clinical research, he investigates how neurodegenerative processes affect emotion recognition, social cognition, interpersonal behaviour, and interoception.

A central objective of his work is to translate fundamental insights into clinically relevant applications. Through close collaboration with memory clinics and international research partners, he aims to bridge fundamental neuroscience and clinical neuropsychology to improve the diagnosis and care of individuals with neuropsychiatric disorders.
