I am a first-year joint PhD student at KU Leuven and UCLouvain, conducting innovative research on socio-cognitive dysfunctions in neurodegenerative diseases. My research integrates cognitive neuroscience, clinical neuropsychology, and psychometric development to investigate social isolation and loneliness.  
I collaborate with international clinical sites contributing to cross-disease projects and multi-site data collection. Our goal is to develop sensitive assessment tools and contribute to personalised approaches that enhance  quality of life in people with neurodegenerative diseases.
