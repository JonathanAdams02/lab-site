**Jonathan Adams**  
*PhD researcher – FWO PhD Fellowship Fundamental Research*

Jonathan Adams is a PhD researcher at the Center for Neuropsychiatry (KU Leuven), supervised by Prof. Jan Van Den Stock. His research is funded by a PhD Fellowship Fundamental Research from the Research Foundation – Flanders (FWO).

His work focuses on the link between the body and social cognition: how the brain perceives signals from inside the body, and how this helps us understand the emotions of others. He combines behavioural experiments and brain imaging to study these processes in healthy people and in neurodegenerative disorders, with a particular interest in the role of the insula.
