After obtaining his MSc in Theoretical Physics from the Vrije Universiteit Brussel in 2010, Laurent professionally moved on to the field of Artificial Intelligence. From 2011 to 2016, he worked as a Research Associate at UGent’s IDLab under the supervision of Profs. Chris Develder and Thomas Demeester, where he was active in a range of projects related to Natural Language Processing. After a short stint in the private sector, he returned to academia in 2018, when he started working as a Research Associate for Prof. Joost Vennekens, focusing on developing AI solutions to industry problems. Three years later, he decided the time was finally ripe to undertake a PhD, but only on his own terms. He brought together KU Leuven’s Prof. Hans Op de Beeck from the experimental psychology Brain and Cognition group, Prof. Jan Van den Stock from the Center for Neuropsychiatry and Prof. Vennekens, and initiated and co-wrote the KU Leuven ID-N project he graduated on in early 2026. His doctoral work is situated in the field of NeuroAI—the interdisciplinary field at the intersection between Neuroscience and Artificial Intelligence—and revolved around the use of Artificial Neural Networks as model of the brain for social cognition. He is currently affiliated as Postdoctoral researcher with Prof. Van den Stock’s lab.
