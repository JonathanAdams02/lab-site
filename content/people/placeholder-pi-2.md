Maarten Laroy is an FWO Junior Postdoctoral Researcher and neuromodulation coordinator at KU Leuven and the University Psychiatric Centre (UPC) KU Leuven. He holds a master’s degree in Experimental Psychology and obtained his PhD in Biomedical Sciences in 2024, investigating neuroplasticity following electroconvulsive therapy (ECT) for depression.

His research combines brain imaging, blood biomarkers and non-invasive brain stimulation to understand how neuromodulation treatments work and improve their clinical and cognitive outcomes. He currently leads RECON-ECT (Restoring Cognition via Neuromodulation in ECT and Depression), which investigates whether transcranial alternating current stimulation (tACS) can improve cognitive functioning in depression and reduce cognitive side effects associated with ECT. The project combines brain stimulation with functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) to examine the mechanisms underlying these effects.

Alongside his research, Maarten founded the new psychiatric transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) ward at UPC KU Leuven, connecting experimental research with the development of clinical neuromodulation services. He is a board member of the Belgian College of Neuropsychopharmacology and Biological Psychiatry (BCNBP), an active member of the Global ECT-MRI Research Collaboration (GEMRIC), and founder and chair of its Young Researchers Committee. He is also active within Stichting Hersenstimulatie, contributing to collaboration and knowledge exchange in brain stimulation.
