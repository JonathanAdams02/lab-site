My research interest covers functional brain network, emotion, depression, and dementia. During my PhD, I focused on alterations in intrinsic brain network, measured via graph theoretical network metrics, and their associations with 11C-UCB-J measured synaptic density in patients with late-life depression (LLD) and patients with amnestic mild cognitive impairment (aMCI).
