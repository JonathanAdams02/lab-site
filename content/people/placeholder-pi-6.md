Filip Bouckaert (°1968) is a Belgian consultant old age psychiatrist and medical director of the University Psychiatric Center KU Leuven, Belgium. He has special clinical and research interest in late life depression and electroconvulsive therapy and is Professor of Psychiatry at Leuven University (KU Leuven), Group Biomedical Sciences, Faculty of Medicine, Department of Neurosciences, Research group of Psychiatry, Laboratory for Translational Neuropsychiatry.
