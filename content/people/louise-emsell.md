Louise Emsell is a neuroimaging scientist, specializing in the application of advanced MRI and PET techniques to investigate brain changes in psychiatric and neurological disorders. Her research focuses on brain ageing and late-life psychiatric disorders, combining multimodal neuroimaging with clinical and behavioural assessments and peripheral biomarkers to understand the neural mechanisms underlying psychiatric symptoms and treatment response. Her work aims to translate neuroimaging findings into clinically relevant biomarkers to improve diagnosis and treatment.
