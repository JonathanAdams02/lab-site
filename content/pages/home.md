themes_title: Research themes
themes:
  - title: Late-life psychiatric disorders
    body: We investigate the neurobiology of psychiatric illness that starts later
      in life, with a focus on late-life depression. Using multimodal PET-MR
      imaging and blood biomarkers, we study accelerated brain ageing,
      inflammation and the motor symptoms that overlap with Parkinson’s disease.
  - title: Social cognition and neurodegeneration
    body: We map the brain networks that support social understanding, and how they
      break down in frontotemporal dementia and other neurodegenerative
      conditions. Functional imaging (fMRI and fNIRS) is combined with clinical
      assessment to turn network findings into clinically useful markers.
  - title: Brain stimulation and neuromodulation
    body: Through the Academic Center for ECT and Neuromodulation (AcCENT), we study
      and refine brain stimulation treatments such as electroconvulsive therapy
      and transcranial alternating current stimulation (tACS), aiming to improve
      outcomes and protect cognition in depression.
